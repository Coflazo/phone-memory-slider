# Composition brief

Phone Memory Slider is shown as the product already behaves: local Android pairing, local analysis, protected favorites, ranked review, and recoverable trash confirmation. The visual language follows the app’s `DESIGN.md`: warm black canvas, hard editorial panels, Geist type, orange action, green keep, and minimal rounding.

Registry primitives informed the per-word rise, physical swipe rail, and low-opacity grain. The final motion remains a single deterministic GSAP timeline so every frame is seek-safe.
