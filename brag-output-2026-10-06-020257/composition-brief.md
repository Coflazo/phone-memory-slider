# Composition brief

Show the real product flow, not a feature slideshow: model-first onboarding, Bluetooth QR pairing, Favorites-led local ranking, swipe review, and Android recoverable trash. Use only synthetic media and a fictional QR payload; no real device address, hostname, gallery, or session secret may appear.

The visual system follows `DESIGN.md`: warm black canvas, Geist type, orange action, green keep, hard editorial panels, restrained rounding, and no decorative cloud imagery. The `chaotic` preset means hard cuts, scan locks, card momentum, and varied entrances—not unreadable copy. Reuse the installed swipe-rail language and the catalog's camera-scan-gate pattern. Keep a single deterministic paused GSAP timeline and a complete poster at frame zero.
