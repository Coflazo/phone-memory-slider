# Brag Plan: Phone Memory Slider

## What is this app?

An offline desktop cleaner that pairs with Android over Bluetooth, learns from Favorites, and puts every deletion behind a human swipe and Android's recoverable-trash prompt.

## The angle

The gallery is chaos; the product is the calm machine inside it. Fast cuts and physical cards create pressure, then the real model-first onboarding, QR scan, and local review flow turn that pressure into control.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)

Three gallery cards crash into frame around the line “Your camera roll, with an exit.” The first frame is already a complete poster.

## Key moments (the middle)

- The real desktop onboarding appears: Pixel selected, private QR revealed, completed checks struck through.
- Favorites become local training signal while a nine-image contact sheet is ranked; uploaded bytes stay at zero.
- Cards leave with physical momentum: likely clutter left, a keeper right, then a video plays in place.
- The final queue moves only after Android shows its recoverable-trash confirmation.

## Outro / punchline

“Private by design. Recoverable by default.”

## User flow worth showing

Name phone → scan desktop QR → allow the full gallery → learn from Favorites → swipe-ranked review → approve Android trash.

## Tone

- Preset: chaotic
- Creative direction: frantic gallery, precise machine
- Interpretation: hard entrances, rapid state changes, strong physical swipes, and one calm final lockup; readable holds remain intact.

## Format: landscape — 1920×1080
## Duration: 24 seconds

## Audio direction

- Role: dense rhythmic layer with restrained UI punctuation
- Music: original locally synthesized pulse
- Music treatment: immediate energy, quiet under readable UI, short fade into the lockup
- Music cue guidance: hard changes at 3.27, 8.74, 11.46, 17.47, and 22.93 seconds
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; UI cards carry the rhythm without waveform decoration
- SFX posture: moderate and motion-matched
- Audio-coupled moments: QR lock, left queue, right keep, trash sheet, logo hit
- Restraint rule: never obscure interface copy or imply an automatic deletion
